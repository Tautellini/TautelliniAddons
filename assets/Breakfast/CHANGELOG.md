# Breakfast changelog

## 1.0.0

- One clickable button for every food, drink, potion, elixir, flask, bandage and scroll in your bags
- Grouped and sorted, with stack count, cooldown and tooltip
- Updates on its own when you loot or use up items
- Movable bar with a lock, button size and buttons per row
- Each group can be turned off
- Shift + right click hides a single item, `/breakfast reset` brings all hidden items back
- Settings panel in English and German
