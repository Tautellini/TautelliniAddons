<span style="color:#f0a020"><strong>All your food, drinks and potions on one bar. No more bag digging.</strong></span>

Breakfast shows every consumable you carry in WoW Forever as a clickable button on its own bar. Loot a new food and it appears. Eat the last one and it is gone. You never drag food to an action bar again.

***

### Features

*   One button for every food, drink, potion, elixir, flask, bandage and scroll in your bags
*   Sorted into groups: buff food, food, drinks, potions, elixirs and flasks, bandages and scrolls
*   Stack count, cooldown and tooltip on every button
*   Updates on its own when you loot or use up items
*   Move the bar anywhere and lock it
*   Turn off the groups you do not need
*   Hide single items with Shift + right click. <code>/breakfast reset</code> brings them back
*   English and German settings

***

### Settings

Find them under Options > AddOns > Breakfast, or type <code>/breakfast</code>.

| Setting           |Default |What it does                                    |
| ----------------- |------- |----------------------------------------------- |
| Six group toggles |on      |show or hide a group of items                   |
| Button size       |36      |width and height of each button in pixels       |
| Buttons per row   |12      |the bar starts a new row after this many        |
| Lock position     |off     |hides the drag handle so the bar stays in place |

***

### In combat

The game does not let addons add or remove clickable buttons during a fight. An item you use up stays greyed out until the fight ends. Loot from the fight appears right after it. Potions and bandages on the bar work in combat as usual.
