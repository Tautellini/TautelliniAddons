# ClearMap changelog

## 1.1.0

New look for all zone maps.

- Mountain ranges are drawn as one solid barrier, so the natural borders of a zone stand out from small hills
- Walkable ground high up on a mountain has its own gold shades, in three steps by height, so it no longer looks like the valley floor
- A slightly more stylized style to fit the game: water in flat bands, harder shadows, shaded mountain edges and a little more color

## 1.0.0

First release.

- 51 zone maps redrawn from the game's terrain: Kalimdor, the Eastern Kingdoms, Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle
- Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Ironforge and Undercity drawn from the floors of their buildings, with district names, also on the minimap. The City maps option turns them off.
- Walkable ground, cliffs with drop-down edges, roads, bridges, buildings, forests, large obstacles
- Water, lava, slime and tar pits
- Cave entrances as pins with the cave's name
- Capital cities highlighted on zone maps
- Area names: Essential or All, uppercase, sized like baked-in map text
- The same map on the minimap, compatible with minimap addons such as EllesmereUI
- All options in a menu on the world map and under Options > AddOns > ClearMap
- Icon in the in-game addon list
