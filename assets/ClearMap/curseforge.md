<span style="color:#3598db"><strong>A clean, modern world map and minimap for WoW Forever. Every zone redrawn from the game's own terrain.</strong></span>

ClearMap replaces the painted zone maps of WoW Forever with clear maps generated from the game's terrain data. You see at a glance where you can walk, where cliffs block your way, where the roads go and where caves begin.

***

### Features

*   51 zone maps redrawn: all of Kalimdor and the Eastern Kingdoms, plus Mount Hyjal, Riverglades, Shen'dralas and Zephras Isle
*   Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Ironforge and Undercity drawn from the floors of their buildings, with their districts named
*   Walkable ground and cliffs, with a heavier line on cliff tops where you can jump down
*   Roads and paths, bridges, buildings, forests, large rocks and fences
*   Water, lava, slime and tar pits, each in its own color
*   Cave entrances as pins, with the cave's name on mouseover
*   Capital cities highlighted on the zone maps around them
*   Area names in two levels: Essential (the areas Blizzard's own map names) or All. Small when zoomed out, larger when zoomed in, like the original map text.
*   The whole map is visible, no fog of war
*   The same map on your minimap, seamless across zone borders
*   Works with minimap addons such as EllesmereUI
*   One click switches back to Blizzard's map

***

### Reading the map

| On the map                       | Meaning                                    |
| -------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------ |
| Light sand                       | walkable ground                            |
| Brown with an outline            | too steep to walk                          |
| Heavier line along a cliff       | the high side: you can jump down here      |
| White line                       | road or path                               |
| Blue, lighter near the shore     | water                                      |
| Orange                           | lava                                       |
| Green pools                      | slime and fel                              |
| Dark pools                       | tar                                        |
| Soft green                       | forest                                     |
| Shapes with a shadow             | buildings                                  |
| Brick red area                   | capital city                               |
| Dashed outline                   | cave passages underground                  |
| Round pin                        | cave entrance, hover it for the cave's name |

***

### On the map

The ClearMap button in the bottom left corner of the world map opens every option, so you never have to leave the map.

***

### Settings

Find them on the map or under Options > AddOns > ClearMap.

| Setting                  | Default   | What it does                                                    |
| ------------------------ | --------- | --------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Clear map                | on        | shows ClearMap's maps instead of Blizzard's                     |
| Capital cities           | on        | highlights capitals and labels them in their own style          |
| Cave entrances           | on        | shows a pin at every cave entrance                              |
| Minimap                  | on        | uses ClearMap's map on the minimap too                          |
| Area names               | Essential | Off, Essential (the areas Blizzard's map names) or All          |
| Label background         | off       | puts a dark box behind names for extra contrast                 |
| Label background opacity | 50 %      | how dark that box is                                            |

***

### Slash command

| Command                   | Effect                     |
| ------------------------- | -------------------------- |
| <code>/clearmap</code>    | turn ClearMap on or off    |

***

### Good to know

*   District names in Stormwind, Orgrimmar, Ironforge and Undercity are in English. The game has no translations of them that addons can read.
*   The minimap falls back to Blizzard's minimap in buildings and caves outside the capitals, in dungeons and raids and while "Rotate Minimap" is on.
*   Dungeon, raid and continent maps keep Blizzard's art.
